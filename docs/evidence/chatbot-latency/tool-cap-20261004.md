# Tool cap: 12 stays, 6 rejected — 2026-10-04

Phase 2 item 4: compare `maxTools: 12` with a smaller cap such as six. Routing
only; no chat-provider request was sent.

## Method

The routing preview (`scripts/chatbot-routing-preflight.mjs`, saved settings
`maxTools` 12, `topSemanticHits` 8, threshold 0.45, Qwen embeddings, dev
server) ran three sets:

- the core routing cases ([json](routing-cap-core-20261004.json)): 18/20;
- the Darija and French cases ([json](routing-cap-darija-fr-20261004.json)): 30/31;
- the 45 benchmark questions that need a tool, from `questions.json`: each
  question's `expectedToolGroups`, plus its blocked write tool as one more
  group, so the refusal can come from the tool ([json](routing-cap-questions-20261004.json)): 44/45.

The misses at the current settings are the known ones (`grade-report-ar`,
`grade-create-es`, `class-count-ary`) and `attendance-mark-blocked-ary`, which
the full benchmark still passes: the model refuses without the tool.

The router keeps, in order, the best match, the other semantic matches (at
most `topSemanticHits`), then their dependencies, and cuts that list at
`maxTools`. A smaller setting is therefore simulated exactly by cutting each
saved `finalTools` list. A smaller `topSemanticHits` is approximated by
keeping the first k matches and the dependencies of those k.

## Result

No question routed more than 10 tools (p50: 8 or 9), so 12 and 10 send the
same tools. Passing cases; a lower setting adds failures to the misses above:

| Setting | Core (20) | Darija/French (31) | Benchmark (45) | Mean tools |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `maxTools` 12 (current) | 18 | 30 | 44 | 8.0 |
| `maxTools` 8 | 14 | 29 | 41 | |
| `maxTools` 6 | 9 | 27 | 37 | |
| `topSemanticHits` 6, dependencies kept | 18 | 30 | 41 | 6.2 |
| `topSemanticHits` 5, dependencies kept | 17 | 29 | 41 | 5.2 |

- **A lower `maxTools` cuts dependencies first.** They come last, so a cap of
  six drops `search_search_students` from every per-student question (grade
  reports, grade and attendance writes, a student's absences or bus) and the
  missing-student checks in all five languages.
- **Fewer semantic matches cost the Arabic answers.** With six, the Arabic and
  Darija class lists and the Arabic missing-student search lose their tool:
  for Arabic the right tool ranks 7th or 8th.

Decision: keep `maxTools` 12 and `topSemanticHits` 8. A smaller tool list
would need better Arabic ranking first (tool descriptions or semantic
phrases), measured the same way.

## Limits

One preview per question; the `topSemanticHits` rows are a simulation, not a
rerun; routing only, so the effect on answers and tokens was not measured.
About eight tools are sent per step; their share of the 3.6k input tokens per
answer (full set, [final run](full50-final-20261003.json)) is the next
measurement (Phase 2 item 6).
